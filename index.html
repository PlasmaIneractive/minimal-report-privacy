Privacy Policy — Last updated: June 2026

minimal.report is a news search application developed by Plasma Interactive. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

minimal.report does not require user registration and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. The app functions as a search tool for publicly available news content.

2. Search Queries

Search queries you enter are sent to Algolia (our search provider) to retrieve relevant news articles. Algolia may log these queries for performance and analytics purposes in accordance with their own privacy policy. We do not store your search history on our servers.

3. Advertising

minimal.report uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. You can learn more about Google's data practices at policies.google.com/privacy. We do not sell your personal data to advertisers.

4. Third-Party Services

The app integrates the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

Algolia — search infrastructure — algolia.com/policies/privacy

Google AdMob — advertising — policies.google.com/privacy

Firebase (Google) — news data storage — firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5. News Content

All news articles displayed in the app are sourced from publicly available RSS feeds. minimal.report does not create or own any of the news content. Clicking an article opens the original source in an in-app browser.

6. Children's Privacy

minimal.report is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at: hello@plasmainteractive.digital
